I’m an MBA candidate at the University of California, Berkeley’s Haas School of Business, graduating in May 2027. I spent summer 2026 as a Summer Associate at Bain & Company in the San Francisco Bay Area, and I’m returning to Bain after Haas.

Before Haas, I spent four years in London and Manchester working on strategy, innovation, and new ventures. At OneLeap, a boutique innovation and entrepreneurship consultancy, I designed leadership programs and workshops, built the firm’s first strategy war game, and helped incubate new ventures for Fortune 500 U.S. and FTSE 100 European clients. Before that, I was a Strategy & New Ventures Analyst at City Football Group, which owns and operates 13 soccer clubs across four continents.
