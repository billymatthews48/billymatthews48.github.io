## OneLeap

London · 2022–2025 · Boutique innovation and entrepreneurship consultancy working for Fortune 500 U.S. and FTSE 100 European clients
{: .experience-meta}

### Strategy Consultant · 2023–2025

- Led a Swiss bank’s flagship leadership program, designing its structure and sessions, then coordinating about 50 stakeholders across five continents during delivery. NPS rose by 30 compared with the previous year.
- Designed, created, and facilitated the inaugural “Leadership in the Age of AI” workshop for 25 cross-industry Kuwaiti executives, drawing on cross-cultural communication skills to win a multi-year contract.
- Pioneered OneLeap’s first war-game simulation to stress-test a Fortune 500 consumer packaged goods company’s sales strategy. I turned market data into intelligence briefs and facilitated executive sessions that identified competitive threats and led to two revenue-protecting measures.
- Co-piloted a direction-setting summit for Fortune 500 consumer packaged goods executives, working with VPs to translate a new strategy into capability-building exercises. Participants rated it 4.8 out of 5.
- Designed a bespoke innovation sprint for the top 50 leaders of a UK company undergoing cultural transformation. Strong engagement prompted the client to expand the scope of the transformation.

### Business Analyst · 2022–2023

- Incubated a new venture for a European bank with more than $1 trillion in assets under management, developing its initial value proposition, business model, and go-to-market strategy. The work led to an expansion contract with the bank.
- Wrote thought-leadership articles on corporate entrepreneurship that supported OneLeap’s value proposition and directly generated three new projects.
- Synthesized senior client interviews and market analysis into a dossier on “25 Trends Shaping the Future of Retail” that informed investment decisions at a FTSE 100 grocer.

Manchester and London · 2021–2022 · Owner-operator of 13 soccer clubs across four continents and provider of consulting services in the sports industry
{: .experience-meta}

### Strategy & New Ventures Analyst

- Developed core components of a five-year strategic plan for an Asian sports league by analyzing market data and building an investment thesis that helped the client secure investor funding.
- Curated a weekly strategic insights newsletter summarizing industry trends for senior leaders. It became standard reference material for key stakeholder meetings.
- Created a first-of-its-kind “Player Value Attribution Model” using web scraping and Excel modeling to quantify portfolio clubs’ financial contributions, prompting the C-suite to reallocate funds.
- Led competitor benchmarking of multi-club ownership groups, analyzing their operational and financial models to guide City Football Group’s expansion strategy. I was selected to present the findings to the CEO and COO.
